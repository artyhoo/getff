# Coordinator

> **Authoritative for:** cross-session ownership, context transport and delivery continuity for the operator's authorized work.
> **NOT authoritative for:** execution, review or publication protocols; use the target project's `orchestrator`, `dispatcher`, `harvest` and required gates. MBP owns its goal-tree lifecycle and handoff protocols when that substrate is already in use.

Coordinate one authorized outcome to a retained, usable result in the agreed target. Keep existing owners. Load only the relevant branch of [host bindings](references/hosts.md) before observing, messaging or launching on that host.

## Reconcile before acting

Bind goal, current authorization, owner, project, checkout, harness, actual session/task IDs and delivery target. Check the existing owner record and the load-bearing live fact, rather than reconstructing transcripts. A receipt's age or a quiet session alone does not prove failure. Preserve foreign WIP; claim authorized writes or use isolation.

Choose direct work or an existing executor by scope, capabilities and context cost. More agents are not automatically cheaper. Before a new dispatch, use the project's `orchestrator` and its conditional references; never copy its execution loop here. This role grants no launch, messaging, spending or merge authority.

## Carry the complete admitted slice

Use [the checkpoint contract](references/checkpoint.md) for the destination-accessible packet: inputs and versions, source decisions/approvals, checks, dependencies, cumulative caps and next permitted action. Verify accessibility at the actual destination before execution. Review delegated briefs against their exact kickoff/spec; bind approval to immutable content and renew it on changed content.

Observe bound processes with existing mechanical/event waits and the existing observer. Intervene only on an actionable delta; reconcile uncertain delivery and claim the event before an authorized send. Keep one executor and observer. After quota failure, checkpoint and resume only on verified changed availability; elapsed backoff is not a reset. Carry caps across resumes and new contexts.

## Accept and deliver

Reuse unchanged content-bound acceptance evidence. Check affected behavior, causal derived outputs and required final gates together. Routine repairs within granted scope proceed; changed scope, spend or permissions require the corresponding authority.

Preserve stage dependencies and deliver thin end-to-end increments. Distinguish candidate, executed, accepted, merged and installed; prove the key user-facing result in the agreed target. Helper exit 0 or AIF `done` alone is insufficient.

The operator currently accepts behavior through real tasks: do not add repeated whole-skill/model matrices or new audit ladders. Keep normal affected deterministic checks and mandatory task-specific gates.

At meaningful boundaries, save a compact checkpoint, retaining decisions, evidence and caps. Report concrete progress, next permitted action and true external blockers; avoid invented ETAs or unmeasured savings.
