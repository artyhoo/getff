## What the substantive material contributes

Pstack is more than orchestration scripts. Its center is a composed engineering style: usage-first types, deep modules, deletion before abstraction, actual user-path proof, scientific diagnosis, explicit scope/roles, and readable human output. Most of those concepts align with getff or already available companions. Alignment matters as evidence of a useful way to express a practice; it does not mean a second workflow owner should be installed.

The original [laziness leaf](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/1e5c90a4b6f249917bd21fe361b3a2a7deb741b8/pstack/skills/principle-laziness-protocol/SKILL.md#L9-L10) has **two body bullets**: consolidate repeated decisions and minimize the solving diff. Flat call hierarchy, questioning signal threading and small representation leaks appear as explicit extra bullets in the [port leaf](https://github.com/mdsmithaustin/pstack/blob/13c863b9c3ca0ddb7ceeab4daca4e69c5021a0d3/skills/principle-laziness-protocol/SKILL.md#L8-L17). Related upstream guidance already appears in [minimize reader load](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/1e5c90a4b6f249917bd21fe361b3a2a7deb741b8/pstack/skills/principle-minimize-reader-load/SKILL.md#L9-L18) and [architect red flags](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/1e5c90a4b6f249917bd21fe361b3a2a7deb741b8/pstack/skills/architect/references/design-red-flags.md#L5-L57). Attribute the exact extra leaf wording to the port without treating all its concepts as independently novel.

Pstack laziness reduces solution complexity. Getff anti-laziness requires complete investigation and evidence. They address different failure classes and can coexist: do all required work while keeping the implementation simple. A smallest-diff rule cannot excuse omitted coverage; an anti-laziness rule cannot justify unnecessary architecture.

Broad simplicity is already available in adopted Superpowers brainstorming (`SKILL.md:214`, YAGNI) and installed MattPocock codebase-design (`SKILL.md:8,20,62-65`, deep modules, deletion test and no hypothetical seam). Getff owns narrower B7 complexity ceilings, H6 public API inflation and H12 dead code. Its current consumer table marks all three `not_wired` ([base-core.md:64,124,130](/Users/art/.codex/worktrees/e220/rules-as-tests-aif/.agents/procedures/getff/references/base-core.md:64)); policy, installed skill availability and enforced check must be discussed separately.

The 24 rows below compare functions, not names. Exact own-core state/carrier/stack exceptions are embedded in the JSON. The table status is installed react-spa primary-trigger status, not a claim all maintainer-side controls are absent. A skill marked `fires` means its command ships, not that it mechanically enforces a principle (`base-core.md:16-33`).
