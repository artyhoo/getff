## F4 — interpretation and limits

Useful candidates are quiet event-driven continuation, error-aware skill formation and explicit attention economics. They do not require adopting a standing roster or replacing AGENTS.md. getff has stronger executable convention/current-review mechanisms than this prompt pack; its remaining end-to-end gap must be tested at ownership/acceptance boundaries (G2/G3), not “fixed” by more role prose. Wrong if applicable installed getff gates fail the named negative examples; then disposition becomes improve, not a claim the field notes shipped enforcement.

Contradictions retained: verification-as-merge-gate is qualified by the real product reading PRs and throwaway code-unread experiment; persistent histories conflict with an “always fresh worker” reading; throughput/PR counts coexist with user-found regressions and zero reported real revenue; chief routing does not establish reduced total operator effort. Sources F-P3/P4/P13/P17 and all failure rows constrain those claims. PA2 uses wrong/stale candidate test; PA5 uses M4; PA1/3/7 use H3, and PA8 uses actual operation accounting. No native Grok capability is inferred from these notes.
