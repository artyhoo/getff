## G5 — interpretation, limitations and PA routes

Existing components provide much of the intended discipline; the load-bearing unknown is continuation of the accountable result owner across host loss, plus consumer closure, not absence of all workflow machinery. This is wrong if an already-enrolled receiver provides the full exact-task repair/recheck/delivery cycle; inspect its receipt before building anything.

PA1/PA3: compare native durable owner route against existing live/resumed getff route with one real finding and owner cut. PA2: retain exact-result intent review and relevant TAK/checks; feed wrong requirement and stale-green candidate to ensure withholding. PA4: use one supported heterogeneous pair with repository/candidate identity. PA7: preserve one meaningful product escalation and pause/cancel. PA6/PA8/PA9 depend on the consumer/host reports. No production component is recommended for retirement; migration/history/rollback therefore remain operator/design work if later proposed.
