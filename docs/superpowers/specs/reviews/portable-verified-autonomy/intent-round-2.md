## Understanding restored

The maintainer agrees a useful development outcome and its boundaries. Available agents then carry it through implementation, meaningful independent review, correction and authorized delivery. The maintainer should not copy results between agents or repeatedly tell them to continue. Task responsibility and applicable sourced experience must survive supported interruptions and agent changes. The same useful capability must work in getff and in consumer repositories, without mandatory new payments or dependence on a single host.

A permitted first result is a bounded task completed through the entire selected cycle, with honest remaining host/consumer/memory proofs. A wrong interpretation is a growing bot roster or successfully submitted task whose outcome still needs human coordination, or whose successful turn is treated as acceptance. A partial pilot cannot be called the whole feature complete.

## What research changes in our understanding

- Ready-made heterogeneous hosts remain useful candidates; their task completion and review-context behavior must preserve getff's product acceptance (V3/V4).
