# Operator requirement for recursive prevention

Date: 2026-11-08. Source: the current chat, after the first architecture candidate was reviewed and before specification agreement.

## C5 Generalize findings into principles and executable prevention

Operator: “еще очень важно не просто проверить и найти но и обобщить и сделать как проверку проверок принцип принципов мета тестирование и TAK чтобы в дальнейшем сразу такое выявлять врубаешься как тест для тестов! как все в этом проекте! это должно в итоге сформулироваться в принцип а принцип в тест - чтобы не дублировалось было эффективно и тд”

Required outcome: generalize the audit's substantiated failure classes into testing principles and executable checks integrated with the existing TAK/metatest system, so subsequent work encounters detection rather than relying on another full manual audit. This is required prevention, not an optional report appendix.

## C6 The same requirements apply to the prevention mechanism

Operator: “Проверки самих проверок тоже должны проходить те же требования - да, замыкающаяся рекурсия!”

Required outcome: the testing principles and prevention checks themselves receive the same relevance, effectiveness, theatre, tautology, duplication, cost-effectiveness and sufficiency assessment. Their addition cannot be exempted because they are metatests.

These statements add product requirements to the approved PRD. They do not establish acceptance of any implementation or approval of an unwritten architecture revision. A finite independent conformance boundary is an architecture proposal, reusing TAK's existing design, not a separate operator quotation.
