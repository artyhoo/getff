## Understanding check

The maintainer wants a complete, evidence-backed assessment of every CI check and test against seven criteria, followed by actual supported improvements. The motivating suspicion is excessive checking, not a predetermined verdict. Required protection must survive simplification; proven unnecessary work must be removed, and useful but disproportionate work must be examined for economical redesign. TAK and metatests remain inside the assessed population. The audit must use the actual relevant repository state and earn its own cost.

The campaign must also generalize demonstrated failure classes into authoritative principles and effective future detection through existing TAK/metatest channels. This prevention receives the same seven assessments, including its own oracle independence, delivery, recurring cost and redundancy. The specification requires mechanically decidable checks plus a named semantic-review path whose invocation and disposition are enforced. It does not claim that an executable presence check proves semantic quality. Independent good/bad cases and reached faults in actual detection or invocation must expose enforcement regressions. The conformance driver has an explicit trusted boundary, is itself assessed, and its claims reopen if that boundary proves unsound.

## Permitted and wrong outcomes

A permitted result is an evidence-backed mix of removal, redesign, repair and retention, with comparable before/after cost and protection evidence, mandatory generalized prevention and visible remaining limits. No safe simplification may be found; that conclusion is permissible only after complete assessment and does not excuse unrepaired confirmed gaps or unfinished prevention. Unknown evidence can limit a claim, but cannot become a successful disposition.

Wrong outcomes include a sampled audit presented as complete; faster green jobs achieved by losing required protection or timing; recommendations without supported implementation; optional retention of proven unnecessary work; principles left only in prose; detectors that recognize only the original incident; a recorded semantic review that nobody consumes; self-certified metachecks; or another full expensive audit on every unrelated PR. The candidate explicitly excludes these outcomes.
