## Permitted outcomes and deferrals

A precise source-bound prerequisite or present-constraints rejection is a valid Q0 finding when the required investigation and output population are complete. Unavailable live account or review-profile evidence remains UNKNOWN/UNVERIFIED and gates the relevant later work; it never becomes PASS. Q1-Q4, useful live semantic/provider proof, production-PC wake/power/access, consumer installation and second-host product proof stay later obligations.
