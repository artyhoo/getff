## Quality and minimality contract

Freeze the required protection before each optimization: fault classes, valid alternatives,
delivery path, supported configurations/hosts, enforcement time and blocking consequence.
Remove proven unnecessary work. Redesign useful disproportionately expensive checks. Retain
necessary proportionate checks. Explicitly resolve uncertain cases; no automatic speculative deletion.
Check combined removals and all retained witnesses, not only each isolated patch.
Generalize root causes into authoritative principles and bounded executable prevention using
existing TAK. Include the new checks and their conformance driver in the same seven-criterion
inventory. Fixed independent good/bad expectations form the explicit trusted boundary; a detector
cannot establish correctness simply by accepting itself. Missing evidence prevents full DONE.

## Review focus

- A and B each called redundant because the other exists: combined change must retain protection.
- A cheap selector silently omits renamed/deleted tests, shared configs or unknown paths: expand safely.
- Nightly or Linux-only replacement loses before-merge or host-specific protection: reject the trade.
- TAK report/driver is old or pending: reconcile state, reuse applicable evidence, avoid duplicate work.
- Prevention recognizes only the original bad example or invokes no real gate: independent variant,
  good companion and detector/invocation fault must establish its bounded claim.

## AI traps and adversarial self-application

Apply `.agents/rules/ai-laziness-traps.md` §2–§3. Active traps: **T3** (actual evidence,
not plausible prose), **T7** (run the category-level adversarial challenge), **T10**
(enumerate before conclusions), **T14** (unknown/unexamined is not certified), **T15**
(self-apply to new metachecks), **T18** (preserve unique protection before deleting),
**T19** (independent acceptance), **T20** (evidence before verdict) and **T22**
(reached fault with a frozen independent oracle).
Domain trap **T-CTE-A**: individually redundant checks may depend on each other; assess
joint removals against surviving witnesses. **T-CTE-B**: a small benefit is not zero;
redesign expensive useful protection without losing required timing or supported environments.
**T-CTE-C**: a registry row or a green metatest is not substantive prevention; test the
real future invocation and detector-disable behavior. **T-CTE-D**: no infinite chain of
new metatests; name the finite independent TAK conformance boundary and assess its cost.

Run this category challenge before each stage report: “Which test or invocation family,
criterion, enforcement boundary, supported environment, or new metacheck have we omitted?
Which combined change can pass our evidence yet weaken protection?” Record the answer,
the resulting bounded probe when needed, and residual uncertainty. Zero findings is legitimate.
A report cannot claim the audit applied to itself without including its new mechanisms and cost.
