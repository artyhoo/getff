## §1 The defect

`inject-matching-rule` ships to consumers who have **no `.claude/rules/` corpus**. With no corpus there is
nothing to match, so the hook does nothing — **forever, and silently**. The consumer sees an installed,
wired hook and has no way to learn it can never fire. A permanent silent no-op is the purest form of this
umbrella's defect class.

## §2 What to build — DECIDED «minimal» (do not redesign)

Corpus absent → the hook **reports ONCE, loudly**. Subsequent runs stay quiet.

- **Never a permanent silent no-op** — the consumer must learn, at least once, that the hook has no corpus.
- **Never per-invocation spam** — a hook that shouts on every edit gets muted, which recreates the silence.

The "once" needs somewhere durable to record that it has already reported; choose the mechanism consistent
with how sibling hooks in this repo already handle session-scoped state, and say which precedent you followed.
