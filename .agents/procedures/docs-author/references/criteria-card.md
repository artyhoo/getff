## How the card is filled

- **By the writer** (per commit, the `Docs-card:` trailer): every id with `PASS`, `FAIL`, or
  `N/A`, or the escape with a reason of at least 20 characters. A self-filled card is a claim;
  the auditor diffs its verdict against it.
- **By the auditor** (per page, the review report): same ids, each verdict with `file:line`, a
  `FAIL` carrying a `Failure-scenario:` that names the reader harm.
- `REVISE` only on a scenario-bearing `FAIL`; everything else is a note. A `FAIL` repeating on
  two or more pages of one kind is systemic — fix the skill or the glossary and regenerate, not
  the page.
